# StileAI backlink kit

Backlinks (other sites linking to stileai.com) are the single biggest lever on Google rankings. This kit is everything you need to go get them: where to submit, and exactly what to paste. No writing required, just copy the blocks below into each form.

Work top to bottom. Each listing is a link back to us and a chance for buyers to find us.

StileAI sits between your team and the AI tools they use every day, and turns your data policy into enforcement. Every decision is logged with sensitive values redacted, mapping to SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001. Start in monitor mode to see your exposure, then enforce with one switch.

## Tier 1: high-value, do these first

## Product Hunt launch checklist

A rushed launch wastes the one shot. Do this:

- [ ] Create a maker account and fill out your profile with a photo and bio.
- [ ] Prepare assets: the logo, the og.png social image, and 3-4 clean screenshots of the dashboard (policies page, audit log, the one-line connect).
- [ ] Write the tagline and description (use the blocks above).
- [ ] Line up a first comment from you as the maker explaining why you built it (the AI data-leak problem, in your own voice).
- [ ] Schedule for 12:01am PT on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday (most traffic).
- [ ] Ask 10-15 people you know to check it out and comment on launch day (genuine comments, not just upvotes).
- [ ] Reply to every comment through the day.

I can draft the tagline, description, first comment, and screenshot captions whenever you are ready to schedule.

## Higher-effort, higher-reward (later)

- **Guest posts / mentions** on security and IT blogs. One article on a respected security site is worth dozens of directory links.
- **HARO / Qwoted / Help a B2B Writer** - respond to journalist requests about AI security and data leaks; land quotes with a link.
- **Comparison and "best AI DLP tools" roundups** - reach out to the authors to be included.
- **Integration and partner pages** - any tool you integrate with may list you.

Focus: a handful of quality links (Product Hunt, G2, Crunchbase, one good guest post) will move rankings more than fifty low-quality directory links. Do Tier 1 well before worrying about the long tail.
