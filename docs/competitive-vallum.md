# Battlecard: StileAI vs. Vallum (vallum.ai)

_Our first identified direct competitor. Use this to sharpen positioning and outreach.
Sources: vallum.ai public pages + press (Aug 2026). Pricing/funding not public — noted as assumptions._

## Who they are

A browser-extension product that governs employee AI usage (ChatGPT, Microsoft
Copilot, Gmail) with plain-English policies to prevent proprietary-data leakage.
MDM-ready extension + Kubernetes cloud backend. HIPAA, AES-256. Founded by
Stanford/Princeton AI researchers with defense-lab background.

**Their motion is enterprise, sales-led:** no public pricing, an `/enterprise` page,
and everything routes to "book a demo." Positioning leans on *enablement* ("surface
real AI use cases") as much as governance.

## What they do well (respect these)

- **Credibility/pedigree** — Stanford/Princeton/defense founders win big-logo trust.
- **MDM-ready rollout** — managed deployment across a fleet (we should match on roadmap).
- **Copilot + Gmail coverage** — broader surface than our v1 (ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini).
- **Enablement framing** — they're not just "the blocker," they're "the AI enabler."

## Where they're weak — our openings

| Their weakness | Our wedge |
|---|---|
| **Sales-led, demo-gated, no public price** | **Self-serve + transparent per-seat pricing** ($25/$59). Swipe a card, live in 5 min, no sales call. |
| **Enterprise-shaped** (procurement, security review, six-figure feel) | **Built for SMB / mid-market IT teams** who can't/won't run enterprise procurement. |
| **Direct-to-enterprise; no channel play** | **MSP channel** — we're already multi-tenant + per-seat + per-employee keys. One console, resell to every client. |
| **Heavy backend (Kubernetes), enterprise onboarding** | **Minutes to value** — invite link → extension → protected. No IT project. |
| Opaque data handling (unstated) | **"We never store your secrets"** — masked audit previews, fail-closed, redaction is a headline. |

## Positioning statement (how we frame ourselves against them)

> **Enterprise AI governance, without the enterprise.**
> StileAI is the self-serve AI security checkpoint for IT teams and MSPs. Block
> secrets, PII, and PHI from leaking into ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — with a
> compliance-grade audit trail — live in 5 minutes, at a price on the website, with
> no sales call and no six-figure contract. Governance for the 95% of companies that
> aren't the Fortune 500.

## Beachhead (IT-focused, per founder direction)

- **Primary:** IT / security leads at **SMB & mid-market (20–500 employees)** adopting
  AI who want control without an enterprise procurement cycle.
- **Channel (high-leverage):** **MSPs / IT service providers** — one MSP resells
  StileAI across its whole book of SMB clients. Multi-tenant is already built.

## Three outreach angles

1. **Anti-friction (vs. Vallum's sales motion):**
   *"AI governance without a sales call or a six-figure contract. See what your team
   is sending to ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini — live in 5 minutes, price on the site."*
2. **MSP channel:**
   *"Add AI governance to your managed stack and resell it to every client. One
   console, per-seat, multi-tenant. New recurring revenue line, zero infra to run."*
3. **The forcing-question (universal):**
   *"If a client or auditor asked you to prove no sensitive data leaks into AI, could
   you? StileAI gives you the control and the audit trail. Installs in minutes."*

## Roadmap implications (to neutralize their edges)

- **MDM/managed rollout** for the extension (they lead here) — priority for MSP deals.
- **Copilot + Gmail/Workspace coverage** in the extension (they have it; we don't yet).
- Keep leaning into **self-serve + price transparency** — it's the thing they *can't*
  easily copy without cannibalizing their enterprise motion.
